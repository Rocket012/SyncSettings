**Your settings. Every version. Every instance.**

SyncSettings is a client-side Minecraft mod for **Fabric**, **NeoForge** and **Forge** (Minecraft **1.20 – 26.x**). It saves your settings into **profiles** that live outside your instances, so every instance and every Minecraft version can start with the same setup.
