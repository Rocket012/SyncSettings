# FAQ

![FAQ](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rocket012/SyncSettings/main/assets/headlines/headline-faq.png)

### Is the mod open source?
No. SyncSettings is closed source (All Rights Reserved).

### Do I need it on the server?
No. SyncSettings is client-side only.

### Can I use it in my modpack?
Yes, see [[Modpacks]].

### Where are my profiles stored?
See [[Profiles#where-profiles-are-stored|Profiles]]. The path is also shown in game.

### Does it work between different Minecraft versions?
Yes. That is the main use case. Changed options are handled by migration rules, see [[What to sync|What-to-sync]].

### A setting was not applied. Why?
Check `syncsettings_changes.log`. It lists skipped values and the reason. Also check that the setting is enabled under **What to sync…**.

### Something went wrong. How do I get my old settings back?
Use **Backups / Undo…**, see [[Backups and Undo|Backups-and-Undo]].
